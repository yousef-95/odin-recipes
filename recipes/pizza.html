Chicken Ranch Pizza

Description

Chicken Ranch Pizza is a delicious twist on the traditional pizza. Instead of using tomato sauce, it uses creamy ranch dressing as the base, which gives it a rich and tangy flavor. It is topped with juicy grilled chicken and melted mozzarella cheese.

Experience a creamy and savory twist on a classic favorite with this Chicken Ranch Pizza. By swapping out the traditional red sauce for a rich, zesty ranch base, this pizza offers a unique flavor profile that pairs perfectly with tender, seasoned grilled chicken. Topped with a generous layer of melted mozzarella and optional crispy bacon or red onions, this pizza is the ultimate comfort food for those who crave something bolder and creamier than a standard slice.

Ingredients

  • For the Base: 1 lb (450g) Pizza dough (homemade or store-bought) 1 tbsp Cornmeal (to prevent sticking)
  • For the Sauce: 1/2 cup High-quality Ranch dressing 1 clove Garlic, minced (optional, to mix into the ranch for extra flavor)
  • For the Toppings: 2 cups Cooked chicken breast, cubed or shredded (grilled or rotisserie) 2 cups Shredded Mozzarella cheese 1/4 cup Grated Parmesan cheese 1/4 cup Red onion, thinly sliced 4 slices Bacon, cooked and crumbled (optional) Fresh parsley or chopped green onions (for garnish)

Steps

  1. and Prep: Preheat your oven to 425°F (220°C). If you have a pizza stone, put it in now. Otherwise, grease a baking sheet or use parchment paper.
  2. the Dough: Roll out your pizza dough on a floured surface until it's your desired thickness. Transfer it to the baking sheet and sprinkle a bit of cornmeal on the bottom for a crispier crust.
  3. Apply the Base: Spread the ranch dressing evenly across the dough, leaving about a half-inch border for the crust. If you have minced garlic, sprinkle it over the ranch now.
  4. Layer the Cheese: Sprinkle a thin layer of mozzarella cheese first. This acts as a "glue" to keep the toppings from sliding off.
  5. the Toppings: Distribute the cubed grilled chicken evenly. Scatter the sliced red onions and crumbled bacon across the top. Finish with the remaining mozzarella and the grated parmesan.
  6. Bake: Place the pizza in the oven and bake for 12–15 minutes, or until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbling and slightly browned.
  7. The Finishing Touch:Remove from the oven and let it cool for 2 minutes. Garnish with fresh parsley or chopped green onions for a pop of color and freshness.
