Burger

Description

The Classic Cheeseburger is the gold standard of comfort food. Featuring a juicy, flame-grilled beef patty topped with melted American cheese, crisp lettuce, ripe tomatoes, and a slice of onion, all held together by a toasted sesame seed bun. It is simple, timeless, and satisfying.

Elevate your dining experience with this Gourmet Truffle Mushroom Burger. This masterpiece combines a premium Wagyu beef patty with sautéed forest mushrooms, melted Swiss cheese, and a drizzle of truffle aioli. Served on a toasted, buttery brioche bun, this burger is a sophisticated blend of earthy flavors and rich textures, designed for those who appreciate a high-end culinary treat.

Ingredients

  • For the Patty: 1 lb (450g) Ground Beef (80% lean / 20% fat for juiciness) 1 tsp Salt 1 tsp Cracked black pepper 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce (the secret ingredient for depth) 1 tbsp Unsalted butter (for searing)
  • For the Toppings: 4 slices of Sharp Cheddar or Swiss cheese 1 large Tomato, thinly sliced 1 head of Iceberg or Romaine lettuce, washed and torn 1 Red onion, thinly sliced into rings 4-8 Dill pickle slices 4 slices of Crispy cooked bacon (optional)
  • For the Bun & Sauce: 4 Brioche buns (toasted with butter) 1/4 cup Mayonnaise 1 tbsp Dijon mustard 1 tbsp Ketchup 1 tsp Smoked paprika (to mix into the sauce)
  1. Prep the Patties: In a bowl, gently mix the ground beef with salt, pepper, and Worcestershire sauce. Shape them into 4 equal patties, making a small indentation in the center of each with your thumb (this prevents the burger from puffing up like a football).
  2. Toast the Buns: Spread a little butter on the inside of the brioche buns. Toast them in a pan over medium heat until golden brown. Set them aside.
  3. Sear the Beef: Heat a cast-iron skillet or pan over medium-high heat with a tablespoon of butter. Add the patties and sear for about 3–4 minutes per side for medium-rare, or longer for medium-well.
  4. Melt the Cheese: During the last minute of cooking, place a slice of cheddar or Swiss cheese on each patty. Cover the pan for 30 seconds to let the cheese melt perfectly.
  5. Mix the Signature Sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together the mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, ketchup, and smoked paprika until smooth.
  6. Assemble the Burger: Spread a generous amount of sauce on the bottom bun. Place the lettuce and tomato slices on top of the sauce. Add the cheesy beef patty. Layer on the red onion, pickles, and crispy bacon. Spread more sauce on the top bun and close the burger.
  7. Serve: Serve immediately while the cheese is still melty and the bun is warm.
