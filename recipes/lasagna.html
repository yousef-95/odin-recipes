Lasagna

Description

Lasagna is a classic Italian dish made of layers of pasta, rich meat sauce, and creamy béchamel. It is known for its hearty taste and comforting feel.

This particular version uses a secret blend of cheeses to make it extra gooey and delicious, perfect for family dinners!

Ingredients

  • For the Meat Sauce (Bolognese): 1 lb (500g) Ground beef 1 onion, finely diced 3 cloves garlic, minced 1 can (28 oz) Crushed tomatoes 2 tbsp Tomato paste 2 tbsp Olive oil 1 tsp Salt and black pepper 1 tsp Dried oregano or basil
  • For the Cheese Filling: 15 oz (425g) Ricotta cheese 1 Egg (beaten) 1/2 cup Grated Parmesan cheese 1 tbsp Fresh parsley, chopped Salt and pepper to taste
  • The Rest: 9-12 Lasagna noodles (boiled or oven-ready) 3 cups Shredded Mozzarella cheese

Steps

  1. Prep the Sauce: Brown the ground beef in a pan, then add diced onions and garlic. Stir in crushed tomatoes, tomato paste, and seasonings. Let it simmer for 20 minutes.
  2. the Cheese: In a medium bowl, combine the ricotta cheese, beaten egg, parmesan, and parsley.
  3. (The Fun Part): Spread a thin layer of meat sauce at the bottom of the baking dish. Place a layer of lasagna noodles on top. Spread a layer of the ricotta cheese mixture. Top with more meat sauce and a sprinkle of mozzarella.
  4. Repeat: Repeat the layers (Noodles → Cheese → Sauce → Mozzarella) until all ingredients are used.
  5. Final Touch: End with a thick layer of mozzarella cheese on the very top.
  6. Cover with foil and bake at 375°F (190°C) for 25 minutes. Remove foil and bake for another 15 minutes until the cheese is golden brown.
  7. Rest: Let the lasagna rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
